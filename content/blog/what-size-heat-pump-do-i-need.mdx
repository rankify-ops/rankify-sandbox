Choosing the right size matters. Too small and you'll run out mid-shower; too big and you're paying to heat water you don't use.

## A quick rule of thumb

| Household | Typical tank size |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 people | 160–220 L |
| 3–4 people | 250–300 L |
| 5+ people | 300 L + |

These are general guides only. Your installer will confirm the right size for your home.

## Things that push you up a size

- Long showers or multiple bathrooms used at once
- Teenagers (seriously)
- A big bath or spa you fill regularly
- Cold climates, where recovery time is longer

## Integrated vs split systems

**Integrated** units have the compressor sitting on top of the tank, making them simple and compact. **Split** systems separate the compressor from the tank, which helps with tight spaces and keeping noise away from bedrooms.
