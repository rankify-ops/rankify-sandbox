If you live around **Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, the Hunter, the northern Central Coast or Wollongong**, you may be able to get a bigger discount on a heat pump hot water system than the rest of NSW. It comes from the NSW Government's **Safeguard Acceleration Program**, it only applies in **60 postcodes**, and it ends in **June 2027**. Here's the full list and how to claim it, using only the [official NSW page](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/hotwater-discount), checked on **20 October 2026**.

## The short answer

- **Larger heat pump discounts** for home owners in **60 Hunter, Central Coast and Illawarra postcodes**.
- The official page **doesn't publish a dollar amount**. It says the discounts are **larger than the standard NSW Energy Savings Scheme** discount (indicative up to $640 replacing electric, $330 replacing gas).
- You must use one of **four accredited businesses** contracted to the program, or an installer subcontracted to one.
- Sign the **nomination form before you pay or install**. You can't get the discount on work that's already done.
- **Install by 30 June 2027**, or earlier if the funding runs out.
- Federal **STCs** still come off on top.

## Who can claim it

The official page says that to get the larger discount you must:

- be a **home owner** in one of the eligible postcodes
- use an **accredited business contracted** to the NSW Government's Safeguard Acceleration Program (some subcontract to other installers, who should tell you which contracted business they work with)
- **complete the installation by 30 June 2027**

A few other points from the page:

- If you **already have an energy efficient heat pump water heater**, you may not be eligible.
- You can claim discounts on **more than one** heat pump water heater, and other eligible appliances, at the same property if you're eligible.
- **Renters:** if you reach an agreement with your landlord, they may be able to claim the discount on your behalf.
- You'll need to make a **minimum payment** towards the cost. Your installer tells you how much.
- You may need to show documents, such as an **electricity bill**, to prove you're eligible.

## The four contracted businesses

On 20 October 2026, the official page listed four accredited businesses, and not every one is active in every postcode:

| Business | Active in |
| --- | --- |
| Ecovantage | All 60 postcodes |
| EC Focus | All 60 postcodes |
| Accredited Power Saver | All except the Illawarra postcodes and 2311, 2330, 2333, 2336, 2337 |
| National Carbon Bank of Australia | All except 2311, 2318, 2324, 2335 and 2336 |

The page says each business will connect you with at least one installer in your area, that installer availability can change with demand, and that you should **get at least two quotes**. It also suggests checking that each installer is offering the discount through the Energy Security Safeguard and that the discount is **shown in the quote**.

We aren't one of these businesses and we don't work for them. If you ask us for a quote, ask the installer we refer you to whether they can offer the Safeguard Acceleration discount and which contracted business they work with. If they can't, the official page links to all four so you can compare.

## How much is the discount?

This is the one thing the official page doesn't tell you. It gives no dollar figure, only that the discounts are "larger than the discounts normally offered by the NSW Energy Savings Scheme". For comparison, the standard [Hot Water Upgrade Incentive page](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/households/grants-rebates/household-energy-saving-upgrades/hot-water-upgrade-incentive) (updated 6 February 2026) lists indicative discounts of:

- **up to $640** when you replace an electric water heater with a heat pump
- **up to $330** when you replace a gas water heater with a heat pump

The page lists what changes your actual discount: the installer you choose, your location and travel costs, the model, how complex your home is to install in, the admin and compliance costs of securing the discount, and the installer's margin. It also says a **more energy efficient model should get a bigger discount**, and that not every model is eligible.

On running costs, the page says a heat pump could save you **up to $800 a year** on your energy bills compared with a gas hot water system, and its FAQ says **up to $600 a year** for a more efficient heat pump. Either way, the savings are per year, on top of the upfront discount. See our [heat pump vs gas running costs](/blog/heat-pump-vs-gas-hot-water-running-costs) guide.

## Federal STCs still apply

STCs are a federal discount that works Australia-wide, as long as the model is on the [Clean Energy Regulator's register](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-renewable-energy-systems/solar-water-heaters-and-air-source-heat-pumps). [Newcastle](/heat-pump-hot-water/newcastle) 2300 and [Wollongong](/heat-pump-hot-water/wollongong) 2500 are in heat pump **zone 3**, so check the zone for your own postcode, especially further up the Hunter Valley. For a 2026 install, the six models we cover earn about **14–16 STCs** in zone 3, worth up to **$560–$640** at the $40 Clearing House cap ([CER](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-technology-certificates/buy-and-sell-small-scale-technology-certificates)). Installers usually sell STCs on the open market, so the real STC discount is often a little lower. More: [how STCs work when you replace your hot water](/blog/how-stcs-work-when-you-replace-your-hot-water).

## How to claim it, step by step

The official page sets out five steps:

1. **Find an accredited business active in your postcode.** Use the search on the [official page](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/hotwater-discount).
2. **Contact one or more of them for quotes.** Get at least two, and check the discount is shown in each.
3. **Choose a heat pump.** Ask each installer which model suits your home. Different installers stock different models, and not every model gets the same discount.
4. **Sign the paperwork.** You'll get a **nomination form**, which must be signed and returned **before you make any payment and before installation begins**, plus a quote showing the work, your discount and the total cost.
5. **Book the installation.** A licensed plumber and/or electrician installs it. They'll take before and after photos, show you how to use the system, dispose of or recycle your old unit, and ask you to sign a **Post Implementation Declaration**.

## If your hot water has already broken

The nomination form has to be signed before any work starts, so the big risk with an emergency is getting a plumber in before you've lined up the discount. If you're in an eligible postcode, ask the first installer you call whether they work with a contracted business **before** they start. If you need hot water fast, see our [broken hot water page](/hot-water-broken).

## Questions to ask your installer

- Are you contracted to the **Safeguard Acceleration Program**, or subcontracted to one of the four contracted businesses? Which one?
- What's the **exact discount** on this model, and is it shown on the quote?
- Does a more efficient model get me a **bigger discount**?
- Are the **STCs a separate line** on the quote?
- What's my **minimum payment**, and when do I sign the nomination form?
- Does the model come with at least a **5-year warranty** covering the heat pump unit and tank? That's the minimum the standard NSW incentive page asks you to check for.
