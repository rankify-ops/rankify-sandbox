Rebates can take a meaningful chunk off the cost of a heat pump. Here's how they generally work. Program rules change often, so always confirm details on the official government pages linked below.

## Federal: Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs)

Under the federal Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme, eligible heat pump water heaters create STCs. The number depends on the model and your climate zone, and the value depends on the market price at the time.

In practice, most installers **apply the STC value as a discount on your quote** so you don't have to deal with the paperwork. Read more at the [Clean Energy Regulator](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-technology-certificates).

## State programs

- **Victoria:** the [Solar Victoria hot water rebate](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/hot-water-rebate) and the [Victorian Energy Upgrades](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/households/victorian-energy-upgrades-for-households) program
- **New South Wales:** the [Hot Water Upgrade Incentive](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/households/grants-rebates/household-energy-saving-upgrades/hot-water-upgrade-incentive) under the Energy Savings Scheme
- **Queensland:** program availability is being confirmed. See [qld.gov.au](https://www.qld.gov.au/housing/buying-owning-home/energy-water-home)
