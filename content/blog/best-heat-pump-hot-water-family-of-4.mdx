A family of four sits right on the line between the two common heat pump sizes. Get it right and you'll never run out. Oversize it and you pay more than you need to. Here's how to choose, using the **manufacturer spec sheets** for the systems in our range.

## The right tank size

Manufacturers size heat pumps by people:

- **270 L** models are listed for **3–4 people**. That's fine for a typical family of four with average showers.
- **300–315 L** models are listed for **4–5** or **4–6 people**. Choose this if you have teenagers, long showers, a bath that gets used or two bathrooms running at once.

Read more in our [sizing guide](/blog/what-size-heat-pump-do-i-need).

## Things that change the answer

- **Where it goes.** Near bedrooms or a neighbour, quieter matters. See [heat pump noise and placement](/blog/heat-pump-hot-water-noise-and-placement).
- **Your climate.** In frosty areas like [Canberra](/heat-pump-hot-water/canberra), [Ballarat](/heat-pump-hot-water/ballarat) or [Launceston](/heat-pump-hot-water/launceston), the rated minimum temperature matters more than price.
- **What you're replacing.** Gas and electric replacements can get different state rebates. See [heat pump vs gas](/blog/heat-pump-vs-gas-hot-water-running-costs) and [heat pump vs electric](/blog/heat-pump-vs-electric-storage-running-costs) running costs.
