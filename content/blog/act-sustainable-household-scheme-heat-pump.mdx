If you own a home in **Canberra** and want to swap your hot water for a heat pump, the main ACT Government help is a **low-interest loan**, not a rebate. The **Sustainable Household Scheme** lends **$2,000 to $20,000 at 3%** over up to **10 years** with no upfront fees. Concession card holders can get a **50% rebate** on top, and federal **STCs** take a chunk off the price for everyone. Here's how it all works, using only the official ACT pages, checked on **21 October 2026**.

## The short answer

- **Sustainable Household Scheme:** loans of **$2,000 to $20,000** at **3%**, repaid over up to **10 years**, no establishment or account-keeping fees, no penalty for paying early ([official page](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/sustainable-household-scheme)).
- **Home Energy Support:** concession card holders who own their home can get **50% of the cost back, up to $2,500**, plus a **zero-interest loan up to $10,000** ([official page](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/home-energy-support-rebates-for-homeowners)).
- **ActewAGL gas-to-heat-pump offer:** **$1,250 off** plus **$250 in bill credits** if you replace gas hot water, but only for installs **by 31 October 2026** ([ActewAGL terms](https://www.actewagl.com.au/for-home/energy-efficient-homes/heating-and-cooling-upgrade/heating-and-cooling-upgrade-terms-and-conditions)).
- **Federal STCs:** Canberra is in heat pump **zone 5**, where the six models we cover earn about **15 to 17 STCs** for a 2026 install.
- The heat pump must be on the **VEU register**, and the ACT recommends a **zone 5 rated** model that runs to **−5 °C** without the booster.

## How the Sustainable Household Scheme loan works

The scheme is run by the ACT Government, and **Brighte** is its loan partner. On the [official page](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/sustainable-household-scheme), checked 21 October 2026:

- **Amount:** $2,000 to $20,000 over the life of the scheme. You can put it towards one product or several, such as a heat pump plus insulation or reverse cycle heating.
- **Interest:** **3%** for households. No establishment fees, no account-keeping fees, and **no penalty for repaying early**. Brighte may charge a fee if you fall behind.
- **Term:** up to **10 years**.
- **Repayments start** once the product is installed and working.
- **Previous participants:** if you applied or were approved before **1 July 2026**, your lifetime limit stays at **$15,000**.
- **Over the limit:** you may be able to borrow more than $20,000 depending on your credit check, but interest may be charged on the extra.

It's a real loan, so you need to meet **standard credit criteria**, including a good credit history and the ability to repay without hardship. Brighte has hardship support if you get into trouble.

### Who can apply

The page says the scheme is open to ACT residents who **own a home** (or hold a licence, for electric vehicle loans only). You must meet the scheme guidelines and Brighte's lending criteria, and **attend a free one-hour workshop**, online or in person.

For hot water specifically, the ACT Government's [heat pump buyer's guide](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/sustainable-household-scheme/buyers-guides/singing-in-the-shower-a-guide-to-hot-water-heat-pumps) adds:

- You must be **replacing an existing gas or inefficient electric system**, so **new builds aren't eligible**.
- The home can be a **standalone residence or a unit titled property**.
- Installation needs an **ACT licensed plumber and electrician**.
- If you need a **3-phase power upgrade**, it can be included in the loan.
- If hot water is your last gas appliance, you can then **disconnect the gas** by plugging the line or removing the meter.

## Which heat pumps qualify

The model must be on the [Victorian Energy Upgrades register](https://www.veu-registry.vic.gov.au/Public/ProductRegistrySearch.aspx) under **"Water heater – Heat pump"**. The ACT guide then recommends three checks for Canberra's winters:

1. a model **rated for zone 5** climates
2. **at least 65% energy savings**
3. it can run **on the heat pump alone down to −5 °C**, without relying on the electric booster

That last point matters more than it sounds. A booster is a plain electric element, and the guide warns it can **significantly increase** your running costs if it does the work on frosty mornings. Here's how the six models we cover line up on the manufacturer's rated operating range:

## Rebates you can stack with the loan

### Home Energy Support (concession card holders)

If you hold a **Pensioner Concession Card, DVA Gold Card or Health Care Card**, the [Home Energy Support program](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/home-energy-support-rebates-for-homeowners) can pay **50% of the supply and installation cost, up to $2,500**, for a hot water heat pump. You must:

- own **and live in** the home
- attend a free online Everyday Climate Choices workshop
- have an **Unimproved Value** of $750,000 or less for freestanding homes, units and dual occupancies, or $300,000 or less for unit titled multi-storey apartments

You can take the rebate on its own (you pay the installer, then claim, and the rebate usually lands in about **10 business days**), or pair it with a **zero-interest loan up to $10,000** through Brighte. Either way, **don't accept a quote until you're pre-approved**. The page is clear on that.

### ActewAGL gas-to-heat-pump discount (ends 31 October 2026)

ActewAGL runs an offer under the ACT's Energy Efficiency Improvement Scheme. Its [terms](https://www.actewagl.com.au/for-home/energy-efficient-homes/heating-and-cooling-upgrade/heating-and-cooling-upgrade-terms-and-conditions) say that if you have an **active ActewAGL electricity account** and replace a **gas storage or instantaneous** system with an eligible heat pump, you get:

- **$1,250 off** installation and **$250** credited to your bill over three years, or
- **$2,000 off** and **$400** in credits for eligible concession holders.

The catch is timing. The terms say the offer covers **installations between 1 July and 31 October 2026**, and the online version only applies through four named retailers on ActewAGL's platform. Electric-to-heat-pump swaps aren't covered by this offer. If you're on gas and can't install by 31 October, check ActewAGL's [heat pump upgrade page](https://www.actewagl.com.au/for-home/energy-efficient-homes/hot-water-heat-pump-upgrade) for whatever replaces it.

### Federal STCs

STCs work Australia-wide for models on the [Clean Energy Regulator's register](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-renewable-energy-systems/solar-water-heaters-and-air-source-heat-pumps). Canberra is in heat pump **zone 5**, where for a 2026 install our six models earn **15 to 17 STCs**, worth up to **$600 to $680** at the $40 Clearing House cap ([CER](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-technology-certificates/buy-and-sell-small-scale-technology-certificates)). Installers usually sell STCs on the open market, so the real discount is often a bit lower. More: [how STCs work](/blog/how-stcs-work-when-you-replace-your-hot-water).

## How to apply, step by step

1. **Check you're eligible.** Read the household guidelines linked from the [scheme page](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/sustainable-household-scheme).
2. **Book the free one-hour workshop.** It's required before you can get a loan.
3. **Get quotes from Brighte-accredited suppliers.** The ACT guide suggests getting **more than one quote**. Ask each for the exact model number so you can check the VEU register.
4. **Pick your quote.** Your chosen supplier starts the loan process with Brighte.
5. **Get it installed.** Repayments start once the system is installed and running.

If you're a concession card holder, apply for the **Home Energy Support rebate before installation** and wait for pre-approval. If you want both the rebate and the zero-interest loan, the whole thing goes through Brighte.

## If your hot water has already broken

The loan goes through an accredited supplier, and the rebate needs pre-approval, so an emergency swap can cost you the help if the first plumber you call isn't set up for it. Before anyone starts work, ask whether they're **accredited with Brighte for the Sustainable Household Scheme**. If you need hot water fast, see our [broken hot water page](/hot-water-broken).

## Questions to ask your installer

- Are you **accredited with Brighte** for the Sustainable Household Scheme?
- What's the exact **model number**, and can you show me it's on the **VEU register** for zone 5?
- Does it run to **−5 °C on the heat pump alone**, or does the booster kick in?
- Are the **STCs a separate line** on the quote, and how many does it create in my postcode?
- Does the price include **removing the old system**, any **switchboard or 3-phase work**, and **capping the gas**?
- What's the **warranty** on the heat pump unit and the tank?
