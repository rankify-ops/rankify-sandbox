NSW homes get two discounts on a heat pump: the state's **Hot Water Upgrade Incentive** and federal **STCs**. Both are usually taken off your quote, so you don't claim anything yourself. Here's what's on offer in 2026, using only the official pages, checked on **17 October 2026**.

## The NSW Hot Water Upgrade Incentive

From the [NSW Government's official page](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/households/grants-rebates/household-energy-saving-upgrades/hot-water-upgrade-incentive) (page updated 6 February 2026):

- **Up to $640** when you replace an **electric** water heater with a heat pump water heater.
- **Up to $330** when you replace a **gas** water heater with a heat pump water heater.
- "The discount amounts provided above are indicative only." The final amount depends on factors such as the installer and the model.
- It's "provided as an upfront discount in the quote provided to you".

### Who's eligible

- "All households and small businesses in NSW may be eligible for this incentive," depending on an installer in your area offering it.
- If you already have an energy efficient hot water system, you may not be eligible.

### The step people miss: the nomination form

The official page says a nomination form **must be completed before the installation begins**, because you won't be reimbursed for work already completed. Installers deliver the incentive by partnering with an Accredited Certificate Provider, and the scheme is administered by IPART. A good installer handles the form with you when you accept the quote.

## Federal STCs in NSW

STCs apply Australia-wide ([Clean Energy Regulator](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-renewable-energy-systems/solar-water-heaters-and-air-source-heat-pumps)). The number depends on the model and your postcode's heat pump zone. [Sydney](/heat-pump-hot-water/sydney), [Newcastle](/heat-pump-hot-water/newcastle), the [Central Coast](/heat-pump-hot-water/central-coast) and [Wollongong](/heat-pump-hot-water/wollongong) postcodes we checked are all in **zone 3**. For a 2026 install of our six models, that's about **14–16 STCs**, worth up to **$560–$640** at the $40 Clearing House cap. Installers usually use the lower open-market price. How it works: [STCs when you replace your hot water](/blog/how-stcs-work-when-you-replace-your-hot-water).

## How to get both discounts

1. **Get a quote from a participating installer.** Ask them to show the STC discount and the NSW incentive as separate lines.
2. **Complete the nomination form before installation.** The official page is clear that you won't be reimbursed for work already done.
3. **Sign the STC assignment.** You assign your STCs to the installer's registered agent in exchange for the discount.
4. **Keep the paperwork.** Your compliance certificate and invoice show what was installed and when.
