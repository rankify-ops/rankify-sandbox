## What "locally made" means

Since 1 July 2025, households that choose an eligible locally made hot water product get a higher Solar Homes rebate of up to **$1,400**, while other eligible products still get up to **$1,000** ([Solar Victoria](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/hot-water-rebate), updated 20 October 2026).

Solar Victoria says products **don't need to be 100% made in Australia**. "Locally made" means the product supports local manufacturing jobs, with **at least 50% locally made or assembled parts**. So "Australian made" in an ad and "locally made" under the rebate aren't the same test. What counts for the rebate is whether the exact model number is on Solar Victoria's list.

## When the extra $400 actually applies

The Solar Victoria rebate is **50% of the purchase price after other discounts**, including STCs and VEU (Victorian Energy Upgrades) discounts, up to $1,000 or $1,400 for locally made products. That gives three cases:

- **Price after other discounts of $2,800 or more:** you get the full $1,400, which is $400 more than a non-local model would get.
- **Between $2,000 and $2,800:** you get half the remaining price, so somewhere between $1,000 and $1,400.
- **Under $2,000:** half the remaining price is under $1,000 anyway, so the locally made cap makes no difference.

The rebate is paid directly to your retailer and taken off your invoice, so you only pay the balance once the system is installed.

## Who can get the rebate

Solar Victoria's hot water rebate is for Victorian householders who:

- are the **owner-occupier** of an existing property where the system will be installed
- have a combined taxable income for all owners of **under $150,000** a year
- own a property worth **under $3 million**
- haven't already received a Solar Homes hot water or solar battery rebate at that address
- are replacing a hot water system that is **at least 3 years old** from its purchase date

You must use an **authorised hot water retailer** and an **eligible product**. Solar Victoria recommends you don't accept a quote in writing or pay a deposit until it confirms you're eligible, and if you pay a deposit, make sure it's fully refundable. Once you're approved, you get a QR code that the installer scans on site before work starts, and the installation should be finished within 120 days. If your system has broken down or is unsafe, you can install first and prove eligibility as soon as possible afterwards, by ticking the emergency installations box when you enter your quote details.

## Victoria's 1 March 2027 gas hot water rule

Solar Victoria's page also notes that from **1 March 2027**, existing homes must replace gas hot water systems with an efficient electric alternative, like a heat pump, when they reach end of life. If your gas system is getting old, it's worth getting quotes now so you can pick a model, rather than taking whatever's in stock on the day it fails.

## Outside Victoria

The locally made bonus is Victorian only. In other states, the federal STC discount works the same whether a model is local or imported, and NSW's Hot Water Upgrade Incentive doesn't pay extra for locally made products. See our [rebates by state guide](/blog/heat-pump-hot-water-rebates-by-state) and [how STCs work](/blog/how-stcs-work-when-you-replace-your-hot-water).

- What's the exact model number, and is it on Solar Victoria's locally made list today?
- Are you an authorised Solar Victoria retailer and an approved VEU provider?
- Does the quote show the price, STCs, VEU discount and Solar Homes rebate as separate lines?
- Is any deposit fully refundable until my eligibility is confirmed?
