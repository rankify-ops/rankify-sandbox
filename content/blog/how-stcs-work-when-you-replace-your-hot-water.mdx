STCs are the federal incentive behind most heat pump discounts in Australia. You rarely see them as a separate payment. They usually show up as a line on your quote. Here's how they work, using the [Clean Energy Regulator's](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-renewable-energy-systems/solar-water-heaters-and-air-source-heat-pumps) own wording where it matters.

## What an STC is

When an eligible solar water heater or air source heat pump is installed, it can create **Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs)** under the federal Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme. Energy retailers need to buy them, so they have a market value. That's what turns them into a discount.

## Is my replacement eligible?

From the CER's pages and guidance:

- The heat pump must be on the CER's **register** of eligible models.
- An air source heat pump must be **no more than 425 L**.
- It must meet **installer licensing requirements** set by your state or territory.
- Replacing an existing system is fine. The CER's [guideline on the information needed to create STCs](https://cer.gov.au/document/swh-collecting-mandatory-information-to-create-stcs) lists installations that replace **an electric hot water system**, **a solar water heater** (including boosted ones) or **a gas hot water system**.

All six systems in our [model range](/models) are on the register. Your installer confirms the exact model code for your install.

## How many STCs a heat pump gets

The number comes from three official inputs ([CER: calculate STC entitlements](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-technology-certificates/calculate-small-scale-technology-certificate-entitlements)):

1. **Your postcode's heat pump zone**, from the CER's postcode zones for solar water heaters and heat pumps (zones 1 to 5).
2. **The model's count for that zone on the register**, which the CER says is "based on the life of the system. This is currently set at 10 years."
3. **The deeming period for the year it's installed.** The CER says it "decreases by one year each year until 2030 when the scheme ends". For 2026 it's 5 years, so the register count is multiplied by **0.5** and rounded down. In 2027 it's 0.4.

For example, the [Sanden Eco Plus 315L](/models/sanden-eco-plus-315) is listed with 34 certificates in zone 5, so a 2026 install in Canberra or Hobart earns 34 × 0.5 = **17 STCs**. For our six models in 2026, the range is about **12–18 STCs**. Try your postcode in the [rebate checker](/rebates), or see every model side by side in the [compare tool](/compare).

## How STCs become a discount

You have two options:

- **Assign them to a registered agent (the usual way).** The CER says: "If you assign the right to create STCs to a registered agent, you can get a discount on the purchase price," and that this "will usually be part of your purchase contract". In practice your installer arranges it and the discount appears on your quote.
- **Create and sell them yourself.** You register as an owner, create the certificates and sell them. The CER warns that registering "could take 6 weeks or longer", and you must still create them within 12 months of installation.

## What they're worth

The CER doesn't set the price agents use. From its [buy and sell page](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-technology-certificates/buy-and-sell-small-scale-technology-certificates):

- "Pricing in the open market is based on supply and demand and can vary daily."
- "Pricing in the STC clearing house is capped at $40, excluding GST." There's "no guarantee on how long STCs will take to sell" there.

So $40 is a ceiling, not a promise. Our estimates show the value at the $40 cap so you know the maximum. Your installer's discount will usually be based on the lower open-market price.

## The paperwork

- **The assignment form.** Agents need an STC assignment form that includes your declaration that you've assigned the right to create STCs to them. The CER's guidance includes owner wording like: "I confirm I assign my right to create small-scale technology certificates against this system for a period of ten years."
- **Proof of installation.** The CER lists a certificate of compliance (or equivalent) from the installer, and an invoice from the installer or a date-stamped photo of the unit.
- **Read before you sign.** The CER's advice: "If you're asked to sign paperwork, ensure you review and understand the documents before signing."
- **The 12-month rule.** "Certificates must be created in the REC Registry within 12 months of the installation date" ([CER: create STCs](https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-technology-certificates/create-small-scale-technology-certificates)).

## STCs plus state rebates

STCs are federal, so they sit alongside state programs. In Victoria, eligible households can also get Solar Victoria's rebate (up to $1,000, or $1,400 for locally made products). In NSW, the Hot Water Upgrade Incentive is an indicative discount of up to $640 replacing an electric water heater, or $330 replacing gas.
